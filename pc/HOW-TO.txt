SWARA PANCHA PAKSHI COMPASS - how to use
========================================

Your bird: set "bird" in config.json (V, O, C, K or P) and your birth Moon sign/star in "natal".
Timetables: pakshi-data.js, generated locally from your own course PDFs (engine/parse_pdfs.py).
Place: Chennai by default. If Windows Location is switched on, the PC follows it.


1. PC POP-UPS
-------------
- A pop-up appears at every sub-period change (about every 12 to 48 minutes).
- Colour bar: green = Excellent/Good, yellow = Average, orange = Weak, red = Avoid.
- Buttons: "OK" closes it. "Snooze 1 hour" silences pop-ups for 60 minutes.
- Quiet hours: no pop-ups between 22:30 and 05:00 (change in config.json).
- It starts by itself when you sign in to Windows.
- Turn off for good: right-click uninstall.ps1 > Run with PowerShell.
- If pop-ups stop, check pakshi.log in this folder.


2. PHONE ALERTS (work offline)
------------------------------
Your phone calendar rings each alert, even with no internet.

One-time setup (Android / Google Calendar), on this PC:
  a) Open calendar.google.com and sign in with the same Google account as your phone.
  b) Left side: "Other calendars" > "+" > "Create new calendar". Name it: Pancha Pakshi.
  c) Open that calendar's Settings > "Event notifications" > Add notification >
     set it to "0 minutes" (at time of event) as a Notification.
  d) Settings (gear) > "Import & export" > Import > choose the file
     Pancha-Pakshi-Chennai-from-...-part1.ics in this folder > "Add to calendar": Pancha Pakshi > Import.
     Repeat for ...-part2.ics (each file holds 15 days).
  e) On your phone, open Google Calendar > Settings > Pancha Pakshi > make sure notifications are on.

iPhone: send the .ics file to yourself (mail or Files) and tap it > Add All.

The two files cover 30 days. To make the next month (or a new town), open a Command Prompt here and run:
    node make-calendar.js
    node make-calendar.js --city Madurai --lat 9.9252 --lon 78.1198
Then import the new file the same way (delete the old Pancha Pakshi calendar first to avoid duplicates).


3. LIVE APP
-----------
The Pakshi Compass page shows the current period, a direction dial, the day strip, panchang,
Ashtakarma windows and the full timetable. Pick your town at the top right.
The app's "Save this app for phone browser" button saves pakshi-compass.html to your phone: open it in Chrome
and, where the browser allows, it uses GPS and the compass automatically.


4. WHAT THE ADVICE IS BASED ON
------------------------------
- Main verdict: the course result (palan) for your bird's yama and sub-period.
- Adjusted by: Padu / Authority bird of the day, Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam, Kuligai,
  Chandrashtamam and Tara bala (from your birth Moon sign and star), rising-sign bird.
- Direction: face your bird's direction when it is strong; face the ruling bird's direction
  when yours is weak. Avoid travelling in the day's Soolam direction and
  against the breath rule (right nostril: avoid West/South; left: avoid East/North).
- Breath (Swara): Shiva Swarodaya rule by tithi at sunrise, switching every hour.
- Ashtakarma: paksha + day/night + facing direction (traditional rules), with the rising
  sign (lagna) and tithi shown. Use only with initiation and for good, protective purposes.

Files here: daemon.js (pop-up service), popup.ps1 (window), location.ps1 (Windows location),
make-calendar.js (phone alerts), config.json (settings), pakshi.log (activity log).
